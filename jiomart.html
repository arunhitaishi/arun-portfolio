Overview

Designing for everyday grocery behavior at scale

JioMart serves a broad spectrum of shopping needs—from planned weekly grocery purchases to urgent quick-commerce orders. This initiative focused on simplifying the end-to-end customer experience while creating a stronger foundation for scalable omnichannel commerce.

Business Context

Grocery commerce requires customers to make frequent purchasing decisions across large catalogs while balancing convenience, speed, trust and delivery expectations.

Project Goals

Improve discoverability, streamline checkout journeys, support repeat purchases and create consistency across shopping experiences.

My Responsibilities

Design leadership, UX strategy, research synthesis, stakeholder alignment, interaction design direction and cross-functional collaboration.

Scope

Discovery, search, category navigation, product evaluation, cart, checkout, order tracking and quick-commerce workflows.

Problem Space

Complex journeys across a high-frequency commerce ecosystem

Discovery Challenges

Large catalogs made product exploration difficult for many shoppers.

Search Inefficiencies

Search often became the primary path to purchase, increasing expectations for speed and relevance.

Checkout Friction

Retention Opportunities

Repeat purchasing behaviors were not consistently supported across journeys.

Reflection

Designing for scale, speed and customer trust

Grocery commerce behaves differently from traditional ecommerce. Customers often arrive with clear intent, familiar products and an expectation of speed. Designing for those behaviors required balancing efficiency with discoverability.

One of the most valuable lessons from this initiative was understanding how search, navigation and fulfillment expectations shape customer confidence throughout the purchase journey.

Success depended on close collaboration across product, engineering, business and operations teams. Aligning customer needs with commercial goals helped create solutions that could scale across a rapidly evolving commerce ecosystem.
