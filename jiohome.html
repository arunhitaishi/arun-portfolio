The Opportunity

Bringing fragmented home experiences into one intelligent ecosystem.

JioHome aimed to create a seamless smart-home platform where users could onboard devices, manage WiFi networks, automate routines, monitor connectivity, and control their home from a single interface. The challenge was not designing features—it was creating clarity across an increasingly complex ecosystem.

The Problem

Users struggled with disconnected experiences across routers, extenders, smart devices, and automation tools. Setup journeys felt technical, navigation was fragmented, and device visibility was inconsistent.

The Vision

Create a premium ecosystem that feels effortless—guiding users from installation to daily automation while reducing complexity and increasing confidence.

Designing the foundation for the connected home experience.
