Case Study

Just Fresh

Product Designer
Grocery Commerce Experience

Designing grocery commerce experiences focused on product discovery, streamlined checkout flows and intuitive order management for repeat purchasing behavior.

Hero Image Placeholder
Overview

Making everyday grocery shopping feel effortless.

Just Fresh is a digital grocery platform designed around speed, convenience and confidence. The project focused on helping users discover products faster, complete purchases with less friction and manage orders more effectively after checkout.

Overview Visual Placeholder
Problem Space

Complex catalogs created friction throughout the journey.

Users struggled to locate products quickly, compare options efficiently and maintain visibility into active orders. Grocery purchases are often high-frequency and task-oriented, making speed and clarity critical to the experience.

Research Insights

Understanding recurring shopping behaviors.

Goal-Oriented Shopping

Most users arrived with a predefined shopping list and expected rapid product access.

Trust Through Detail

Product information, pricing and availability directly influenced purchase confidence.

Post-Purchase Visibility

Order tracking and status updates were key drivers of satisfaction after checkout.

Design Strategy

Reduce effort across every stage of the shopping flow.

The design strategy centered on improving findability, simplifying purchasing decisions and creating transparency throughout the order lifecycle.

Key UX Improvements

Focused enhancements across the core journey.

Discovery

Improved category structures and surfaced relevant recommendations for faster browsing.

Search

Refined search patterns with predictive suggestions and clearer result prioritization.

Product Pages

Enhanced pricing visibility, product details and purchasing actions to support decision making.

Checkout

Reduced unnecessary steps and simplified address, payment and order review interactions.

Order Management

Introduced clearer tracking states, delivery updates and order history access.

UX Improvements Mockup Placeholder
Outcomes

Improving efficiency and customer confidence.

Faster Product discovery experience
Simpler Checkout completion flow
Clearer Order tracking visibility
Better Overall shopping experience
Reflection

Designing for frequency and convenience.

Grocery commerce differs from many retail experiences because users often return multiple times each week. This project reinforced the importance of reducing cognitive load, prioritizing speed and creating trust through transparency. Small usability improvements across discovery, checkout and order management can significantly improve the overall customer experience.
