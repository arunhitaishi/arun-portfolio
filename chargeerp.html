Enterprise UX Case Study

chargeERP

Designing enterprise workflow experiences for billing, operations and business management.

Role
Product Designer
Product
Enterprise Workflow Platform
Focus
Systems Thinking • Workflow Design • Enterprise UX
Overview

Building a platform that helps businesses manage operations, billing and organizational workflows at scale.

chargeERP was designed to support complex enterprise operations, streamline business processes and improve visibility across teams. Collaboration with product, engineering and business stakeholders ensured workflows aligned with real operational needs.

Challenge

Reducing friction across highly complex enterprise workflows.

Complex Workflows

Enterprise processes required users to navigate multiple interconnected tasks and systems.

Information Density

Large volumes of operational data created cognitive burden and reduced discoverability.

User Roles & Permissions

Different teams required tailored experiences based on responsibilities and access levels.

Operational Inefficiencies

Manual processes and fragmented interfaces slowed execution and decision making.

Research & Insights

Understanding enterprise behaviors before designing solutions.

User Interviews

Conducted interviews across operational, finance and management teams to identify workflow bottlenecks and recurring pain points.

Process Mapping

Visualized end-to-end business processes to uncover dependencies, inefficiencies and system gaps.

Stakeholder Workshops

Facilitated alignment sessions with business leaders and product teams to define priorities and success metrics.

Workflow Analysis

Evaluated task flows and information architecture to identify opportunities for simplification.

Design Strategy

Designing systems that scale while making complexity feel manageable.

Simplify Enterprise Complexity

Structured experiences around user goals rather than system architecture.

Improve Discoverability

Enhanced navigation patterns and information hierarchy for faster task completion.

Reduce Cognitive Load

Introduced progressive disclosure and contextual actions to improve usability.

Create Scalable Patterns

Established reusable design foundations that supported future growth and consistency.

Key UX Initiatives

Core initiatives focused on productivity, visibility and operational control.

Dashboard Redesign

Surfaced critical information through role-focused dashboards and actionable insights.

Workflow Management

Streamlined complex processes into clearer, task-driven experiences.

Reporting Experiences

Improved access to operational and financial reporting capabilities.

Data-Heavy Interfaces

Enhanced readability and scanning through improved hierarchy and visual organization.

Role-Based Access

Tailored experiences and permissions around organizational responsibilities.

Operations Management

Created tools that improved visibility into daily business activities and workflows.

Outcomes

Delivering a more efficient and scalable enterprise experience.

Reflection

Enterprise design is ultimately about helping people navigate complexity with confidence.

This project reinforced the importance of systems thinking, stakeholder alignment and scalable design patterns. By focusing on workflows instead of screens, the experience evolved into a platform capable of supporting growing operational demands while remaining intuitive for everyday users.
