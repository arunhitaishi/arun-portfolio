Bijak

Transforming India's agricultural trade ecosystem through digital products. Designing trust, transparency, credit, and payments experiences for one of the country's largest agricultural commerce platforms.

Role Senior Product Designer
Industry AgriTech & B2B Commerce
Focus Marketplace, Credit & Payments
Hero Product Showcase / Marketplace Experience
Overview

Modernizing agricultural trade.

Bijak operates at the intersection of agriculture, commerce, logistics, payments, and credit. The platform enables buyers, sellers, and commission agents to conduct trade digitally, reducing friction in traditionally offline workflows.

As a Senior Product Designer, I led end-to-end UX initiatives across marketplace experiences, trade management systems, financial products, and payment journeys.

Business Context

India's agricultural supply chain involves multiple stakeholders, fragmented communication channels, and operational inefficiencies that impact scale.

Responsibilities

Product strategy, UX design, interaction design, workflow optimization, stakeholder collaboration, prototyping, validation, and design delivery.

Challenge

Building trust at scale.

Agricultural commerce often relies on relationships, manual processes, and limited visibility into transactions. The challenge was to create experiences that improve confidence, efficiency, and transparency across every stage of the trade lifecycle.

Fragmented Workflows

Multiple disconnected tools and manual processes slowed down procurement, inventory management, and trade execution.

Trust & Transparency

Buyers and sellers required greater visibility into transactions, order status, and partner credibility.

Credit Complexity

Accessing trade credit involved operational friction and unclear decision-making pathways.

Payments

Payment workflows required improved clarity, reliability, and confidence throughout the journey.

Research & Insights

Understanding every stakeholder.

Through stakeholder interviews, workflow mapping, operational reviews, and collaborative product discovery, we identified recurring pain points and opportunities across the ecosystem.
Research Synthesis / Journey Maps / Workflow Analysis

Buyers

Needed faster sourcing decisions, transparent pricing, reliable trade records, and easier payment management.

Sellers

Required confidence in buyers, improved visibility, and streamlined operational processes.

Commission Agents

Acted as critical ecosystem facilitators and needed efficient trade management capabilities.

Operations Teams

Needed scalable workflows that reduced manual effort and improved information accessibility.

Design Strategy

Simplify trade. Increase confidence.

The design strategy focused on creating a cohesive ecosystem where marketplace discovery, financial services, and operational management worked together as a seamless product experience.

Reflection

Designing for an industry at national scale.

Working on Bijak reinforced the importance of balancing business goals, operational realities, and user needs within highly complex ecosystems. The project required deep cross-functional collaboration across product, engineering, operations, finance, and leadership teams.

Beyond interface design, the work focused on creating trust, reducing friction, and enabling better outcomes for thousands of stakeholders participating in India's agricultural trade network.
