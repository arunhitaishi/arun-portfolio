Overview

Making software creation accessible to everyone.

Builder.ai enables entrepreneurs and businesses to create software products without requiring deep technical expertise.

I worked on customer-facing dashboards, project visibility, workflow management, and communication experiences while collaborating closely with product, engineering, and business stakeholders.

Product Overview
Challenge

Turning complex software development into a simple customer experience.

Limited Visibility

Customers often struggled to understand project progress and delivery status.

Workflow Complexity

Development processes contained technical concepts that felt intimidating to non-technical users.

Trust & Confidence

Users needed greater transparency and reassurance throughout the product-building journey.

Reflection

This project reinforced the importance of designing for users who may not understand the underlying technical systems. The challenge was not adding more information—it was presenting complexity in a way that felt approachable, transparent and trustworthy while balancing both customer and business goals.
